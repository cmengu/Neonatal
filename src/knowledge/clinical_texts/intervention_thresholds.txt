[Source: NICE-NG195; AAP-PRETERM-2018] Immediate clinical review is warranted when a neonate shows a sustained RMSSD z-score below -2.5 from personal baseline together with two or more bradycardia events in the preceding 6 hours. This is an adjunct risk trend, not a diagnosis, and its predictive performance is unmeasured on this unlabelled cohort. Blood culture and CBC with differential are a clinician decision at the bedside — the HRV signal prompts the review; it cannot itself justify a culture, and laboratory tests alone are neither sensitive nor specific for early-onset sepsis (AAP).
Category: intervention_thresholds. Risk tier: RED.

[Source: NICE-NG195] Continue observation on a newborn early-warning system when a single HRV feature shows a z-score deviation between -2.0 and -2.5 from personal baseline without other concurrent features changing. Single-feature mild deviations can reflect positional changes, feeding state, or sleep-state transitions rather than pathology. If the deviation persists or worsens, escalate to clinical review; if it normalises, continue routine monitoring. NICE NG195 specifies monitoring on a newborn early-warning system for at least 12 hours for a single non-red-flag risk factor.
Category: intervention_thresholds. Risk tier: YELLOW.

[Source: NICE-NG195; NG-METHOD] Continue routine monitoring at the standard frequency when all HRV z-scores remain within 1.5 standard deviations of the patient's personal baseline and no bradycardia events have occurred in the preceding 6 hours. Document baseline stability in the patient record. Routine monitoring for premature neonates under 32 weeks is continuous HRV assessment with alerts reviewed on the unit's standard schedule.
Category: intervention_thresholds. Risk tier: GREEN.

[Source: NICE-NG195; NG-METHOD] Increase monitoring frequency when HRV shows a directional trend: any two features moving consistently toward their alert thresholds across three or more consecutive windows, even if individual values remain within 2 standard deviations of baseline. Trends that persist for 4 or more hours without reaching alert thresholds should be flagged to the attending neonatologist for awareness even if no immediate action is indicated.
Category: intervention_thresholds. Risk tier: YELLOW.
